WHY IT MATTERS

External crises may one day end, but anxiety, brokenness, and uncertainty often remain in the daily lives of families and young people.

ABOUT US

About Us

Chinese Silk Road Cultural Exchange Association is a legally registered nonprofit association in Taiwan, whose mission is to promote cultural and educational exchange between Taiwan, the countries along the Silk Road, and the world. Through the "Green Olive" youth companionship project and the "Anna" single-parent family care project, the association walks long-term with young people and single-parent families living in the shadow of war — through financial support, in-depth home visits, life-growth fellowship, and persistent prayer — so that hope enters real, lived circumstances.
